Privacy Policy — PhotoCleaner

Last updated: 13 August 2026

PhotoCleaner (“the app”) is developed by KTN Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your photos never leave your device through us.

1. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, or transmit any personal data. We operate no backend service, and the app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not create user accounts and never ask for your name, email address, or any other identifier.

2. Photos and videos

The app requests access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos, find duplicates, similar shots, blurry images, screenshots, and large files, and let you delete or compress them.

  • All analysis runs on your device, using Apple’s on-device frameworks (Photos and Vision). Nothing is uploaded.
  • We never copy, transmit, or retain your photos or videos.
  • Deletions go through the system photo library, which moves items to your device’s “Recently Deleted” album, where iOS keeps them for about 30 days before permanently removing them.
  • Compression writes a new copy back into your library only when you ask for it.
  • You can grant full or limited library access, or revoke it entirely, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores its own working data locally: your cleaning decisions (which items you kept or discarded), review progress, session statistics, and app preferences such as language and reminder settings. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

4. iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, a small amount of that working data — cleaning decisions and progress, not photos — is stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple’s iCloud key-value store, so your progress carries across your devices. This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. Sync is off unless you turn it on and can be disabled at any time.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. These are generated locally by iOS. No push server is involved and no device token is sent to us.

6. App Store and ratings

The app may show Apple’s standard in-app rating prompt. Any review you write, and any purchase or download data, is handled by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the aggregate, anonymous statistics Apple provides to all developers in App Store Connect; these cannot identify you.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete under the GDPR, CCPA, or comparable laws. All app data lives on your device and in your own iCloud account, and you remove it by deleting the app and, if you used iCloud sync, by clearing the app’s iCloud data in your device settings.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
