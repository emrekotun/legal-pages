# Privacy Policy — Video Compressor

**Effective date:** 6 August 2026
**Contact:** ktnapps@gmail.com
**App:** Video Compressor for iOS (`com.ktnapps.video-compressor-ios`)

---

## Summary

Video Compressor does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Every video you process stays on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect, receive, or have access to any information about you or your device. We cannot see your videos, your file names, your usage, or your identity.

## Data processed on your device only

The app stores the following locally on your iPhone or iPad. This data is never uploaded and is not accessible to the developer:

- **Videos you select.** Read from Photos or Files only after you pick them, processed with Apple's on-device AVFoundation engine, and written to the app's own storage.
- **Compressed output files.** Kept in the app's storage until you save, share, or delete them. You can delete all of them at any time in Settings → Storage → Clear Compressed Files.
- **Compression history.** File names, sizes before and after, and settings used. Deletable in Settings → Storage → Clear History.
- **App preferences and statistics.** Default quality, saved presets, haptics setting, and total space saved. Stored in your device's standard app preferences and shared with the app's own widget and Share extension via an app group container. Removed when you delete the app.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It does not contact our servers because we operate none, and it does not contact third-party services. It functions fully in Airplane Mode.

## Permissions and why they are requested

- **Photos (add).** Requested only when you save a compressed video back to your photo library. Requested only at that moment, and the app works without it — you can save to Files instead.
- **Photos (read).** Videos you choose through the system picker are handed to the app by iOS. The app does not browse or index your library.
- **Notifications.** Optional. Used only to tell you a batch finished compressing. Notifications are generated on your device; no push server is involved.
- **Background processing.** Used only to continue compressing videos you already started while the app is in the background.

Any permission can be revoked at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, crash reporters, or trackers. No data is shared with anyone because no data is collected.

Apple may collect its own information when you download the app or send feedback through the App Store. That processing is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by us. If you email us for support, we receive only what you write in that email and use it solely to answer you.

## Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Our App Store privacy label declares **Data Not Collected**.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children. It complies with COPPA and the GDPR by design.

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / KVKK)

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase on your behalf. All data described above lives on your device and is fully under your control: delete individual items in the app, clear everything in Settings, or delete the app to remove all of it at once.

## Data retention

We retain nothing. On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Security

Your data never leaves your device, which removes the risk of interception or server breach. On-device files are protected by iOS file-level encryption and app sandboxing.
