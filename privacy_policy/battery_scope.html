BatteryScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

BatteryScope is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate servers, accounts, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks.

Data we collect

None. Battery readings, charge sessions, health values you enter, and exports are stored locally on your iPhone.

Data sharing

We do not transmit your battery data. If you use Export or Share, you choose where files go.

Widgets & App Groups

Battery level and charging estimates are written to an App Group container shared only between BatteryScope and its widget extension on your device.

Notifications

Local alerts (charge limit, low battery, full charge) are scheduled on-device. No push notification servers are used.

Children

BatteryScope is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Updates are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
