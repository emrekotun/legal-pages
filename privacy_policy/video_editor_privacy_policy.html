Privacy Policy

ReelCut Video Editor · Last updated 3 August 2026

ReelCut does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Your videos, photos, and audio never leave your device through ReelCut.

Data we collect

None. ReelCut contains no server, no login, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own, so there is nothing for us to receive, store, or share. We cannot see your projects, your media, or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Data Where it lives
Projects, imported clips and photos, exported videos The app's private storage area on your device, inside the app sandbox
Voiceover recordings The same project storage, alongside the project they belong to
Caption text generated from your audio Saved in the project file as text overlays
App preferences (for example haptics on or off) Standard iOS app preferences storage
Crash and performance diagnostics from Apple's MetricKit Stored locally under Application Support and never uploaded. They contain Apple-provided metrics only — no media, no project names

Deleting a project removes its media from the app's storage. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photo Library (read) — to import the videos and photos you pick for a project. ReelCut only receives the items you select.
  • Photo Library (add) — to save an exported video back to your library when you ask it to.
  • Microphone — to record a voiceover onto the timeline. Recording only happens while you use the voiceover feature.
  • Speech Recognition — to turn a project's audio into captions. ReelCut requires on-device recognition and will refuse to transcribe if the selected language cannot run on-device, so clip audio is never sent to Apple's servers for this feature.

Each permission is requested only when you first use the feature that needs it, and every one of them can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. Denying a permission disables that feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

Face data and biometric data

ReelCut does not collect, generate, process, store, or transmit face data or any other biometric data. The app performs no face detection, face tracking, face recognition, face landmark or face mesh analysis, face-based effects, beautification, face filters, or age or emotion estimation. It does not use ARKit face tracking, the TrueDepth camera, Face ID authentication, the Vision framework's face APIs, or any comparable technology, and it contains no third-party SDK that does. The app has no camera capture feature at all: video and photos are imported only from the items you select in your Photo Library, and they are treated purely as image and audio frames for editing.

Because no face data is ever created, none is shared with third parties, none is stored anywhere (on device or on any server), and there is no retention period — the retention period is zero. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before such a feature ships, and the app will ask for your explicit consent.

Sharing and third parties

ReelCut has no third-party SDKs and shares nothing with anyone. When you tap Share or Save, iOS hands your exported file to the destination you choose — Photos, Messages, another app. What happens after that is governed by that app or service's own privacy policy, not this one.

Apple may collect information about app installs, crashes, and purchases through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. That relationship is between you and Apple.

Children

ReelCut is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request. All of your ReelCut data is under your control on your own device. If you have a question about this, email us and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
