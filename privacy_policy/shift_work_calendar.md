## Privacy Policy

**Shift Work Calendar** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Shift Work Calendar mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information We Collect

Shift Work Calendar is designed with an **offline-first** and **privacy-by-design** approach.

We do **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

Specifically, Shift Work Calendar does **not** collect:

* Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, address, etc.)
* Account information (no account or login is required)
* Usage analytics or tracking data
* Location data
* Advertising identifiers

All data you enter into the App (shift schedules, rotations, calendar entries, reminders, statistics, notes) is stored **locally on your device only**.

---

### 2. How Your Data Is Used

Any data you create in Shift Work Calendar is used solely to provide the App’s core functionality, including:

* Creating and managing shift calendars (14/14, 7/7, 4-2-4-4, custom rotations)
* Displaying schedules in Month, Week, and Year views
* Generating work-life statistics (work days, night shifts, time off)
* Creating reminders for upcoming shifts
* Exporting calendars and reports as **PDF** files
* Backing up or transferring data **only when you explicitly choose to export it**

Shift Work Calendar does **not** access your data for any other purpose.

---

### 3. Data Sharing

We do **not** share your data with third parties.

Shift Work Calendar:

* Does not use third-party analytics SDKs
* Does not display ads
* Does not use advertising or tracking frameworks
* Does not upload data to cloud servers

Your data never leaves your device unless **you manually export or share it** (for example, sharing a PDF calendar with family).

---

### 4. Permissions

Shift Work Calendar may request limited system permissions strictly to support core features:

* **Calendar / Notifications** – to deliver shift reminders
* **File Storage** – to export PDF calendars or backup files

These permissions are used only on-device and are never used to collect or transmit personal data.

---

### 5. Offline-First Design

Shift Work Calendar works **fully offline**:

* No internet connection is required
* No background network activity occurs

This ensures reliability for professionals working in remote or secure environments such as aviation, maritime, mining, healthcare, and emergency services.

---

### 6. Data Backup & Transfer

Shift Work Calendar allows you to export your data for backup or transfer purposes.

* Backups are created **only when you initiate them**
* Exported files remain under your control
* We do not have access to exported data

---

### 7. Children's Privacy

Shift Work Calendar is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

---

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the App Store listing or within the App.

Continued use of Shift Work Calendar after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
